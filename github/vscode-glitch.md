Hi there!

There could be multiple reasons.
I'll keep it straight.

### 1. Quite VSCode completely, if it is still running in background then:

- Go to Task Manager.
- Find Vs Code.
- Terminate.

### 2. After Terminating, restart your PC.

Why? It happens A LOT with me, because my PC's ram causes issues. When you restart your PC, try to make sure to NOT open anything except VsCode, and do what you were suppose to do.

### 3. Press Ctrl + Shift + P — Type in "Reload Window" then → Enter.

It refreshes everything.

Your case scenario, doesn't have issues regarding, naming files, or changing folder structures BUT many times deletion doesn't work as intended either. In either case, it's mostly RAM's issue (not that your RAM is faulty but it means there's not enough space to breath, you can usually see that in C:/)
